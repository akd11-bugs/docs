It is quick, intuitive, and regularly monitored by our expert Client Resolution Team to ensure timely and effective issue resolution. ### How to raise a ticket ? In order to raise a ticket please navigate to your merchant dashboard here on the left sidepane click the option "Help". Upon clicking you will see two options one of which says "Tickets". Upon selecting you will be taken to your ticket system. There you can do the following : 
1\. Raise Ticket with short/long description and attach snapshot of your issue to help better understand. 2. Mointor your ticket status. 3. Close the issue. \*\*\* 1. Step 1 - Navigate to your dashboard and login with merchant account.

![](https://gofastflowpe.gitbook.io/files/vlv7DpDvEjHPLbgO10Yx)

2\. Choose "Help" from the left sidepane and select "Tickets".

![](https://gofastflowpe.gitbook.io/files/XXUQJyDHOGZiH7VcPEq5)

3\. Once you are at your ticket centre, select the button "Create Ticket" in blue.

![](https://gofastflowpe.gitbook.io/files/5dLA0WPPwlL0kV0vwiKC)

4\. When you click the button a box to raise a new ticket opens, please fill in: 1. A ticket heading. 2. A short description. 3. A long description of max of 250 words. 4. A snapshot or a document that you would to go with the ticket.

![](https://gofastflowpe.gitbook.io/files/BaT6z3YzlXcqbxkMmsXm)

5\. Upon successfully creation you will be given a unique ticket code that you can use to track your ticket and mail for the same will be automatically sent to your registered email address.
